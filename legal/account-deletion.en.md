# How to Delete Your Nona Account

**Effective:** August 25, 2026
**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

Nona is part of **Juegos Senda**, a family of games by SENDA LLC that share a
single account. That is why deleting has **two distinct meanings**, and the App
offers you both of them separately:

- **Remove Nona from your account** — your Nona progress goes. You keep your
  account, your @username, your friends and your progress in the other games.
- **Delete your Juegos Senda account** — everything goes, across **every** game
  in the family, including the ones you do not have installed on this phone.

This page explains how to do each, exactly what is deleted, what survives, and
how long it takes.

> **Playing without an account?** Then there is nothing to delete from our
> servers: we create no profile and store none of your games. Everything lives
> on your phone and disappears when you uninstall the App.

---

## How to reach the two options

1. Open Nona and sign in.
2. Go to the **More** tab.
3. Tap **your profile card** (the one showing your avatar and your @username).
4. At the bottom of the sheet that opens, tap **Delete account**.
5. You will see **both options**, each with its own explanation. Choose the one
   you want.
6. To confirm, you will have to **type your @username**. That is deliberate:
   these are actions that cannot be undone, and typing it out prevents an
   accidental tap.

The App may ask you to sign in again with Google or Apple before completing a
full deletion. It is not a redundant step: it is the security check the account
system requires when your session has been open for a while, and it exists so
that nobody who picks up your unlocked phone can delete your account.

### If you cannot get into the App

If you have lost access to your Google or Apple account, or cannot use the App
for any other reason, write to us at **support@sendapr.com** from the email
address associated with your account, with the subject line:

```
Account deletion request — Nona
```

Tell us whether you want to **leave Nona only** or **delete your entire Juegos
Senda account**. We process these requests **within 30 days** of receiving
them, and usually within the first few. We confirm by email when it is done.

## Option 1 — Remove Nona from your account

This is what the App calls **"Remove Nona from your account"**.

### What is deleted

- **Your Nona progress**: your streak, your best streak, your points — total,
  weekly and monthly —, the record of every daily game you played, and today's
  if you left it half played.
- **Your presence in the Nona leaderboard**: you stop appearing in your
  friends' standings.
- **The marker showing that you have Nona**: your friends stop seeing you as a
  Nona player, and will be able to invite you again.
- **Your session in this game**: when it finishes, the App signs you out here.

### What you keep

- **Your Juegos Senda account** and your Google or Apple sign-in.
- **Your @username**, which remains yours and is not released.
- **Your display name, your avatar and your country.**
- **Your friends**, across every game.
- **Your progress in the other games** in the family.

You can **come back to Nona whenever you want**. You would start from zero in
this game, with the same identity as always.

---

## Option 2 — Delete your Juegos Senda account

This is what the App calls **"Delete everything"**. It is **immediate and
irreversible**.

### What is deleted

The following is permanently removed from our servers, **across every game in
the family**:

- **Your profile**: display name, avatar, country and creation date.
- **All of your progress**: streaks, points and every one of your games, in
  **all** Juegos Senda games — **including those you do not have installed on
  this phone**.
- **Your friendships and pending requests**, in both directions. Those who were
  your friends stop seeing you in their list and in their leaderboards.
- **Your block list.**
- **Your notification tokens**, so you stop receiving alerts.
- **Your access record**, that is, the entry linking your Google or Apple
  sign-in to Juegos Senda.
- **Your "Sign in with Apple" authorization**, if that is how you signed in.
  When the account is deleted we ask Apple to **revoke the permission** you
  granted.

### Your @usernames are released

When you delete the account, your **@username becomes available again** to
anybody else, **and so do any you had before**, if you ever changed it. If you
later create a new account and that name has already been
claimed, you will have to choose another one: we do not reserve it.

That is the main difference from option 1: **only full deletion releases your
@usernames**.

### About the Apple permission: it appears as "CINCO"

If you signed in with Apple, there is a detail worth knowing so that it does not
confuse you: under **Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple**, the
authorization **does not appear as "Nona", but as "CINCO"**.

This is not a mistake. Juegos Senda games share a single Apple sign-in, and the
system lists it under the name of the first game in the family. It is the same
authorization for all of them, which is precisely what makes your account the
same across every one.

Normally you do not have to do anything: the App asks Apple to revoke it for
you. If that revocation were to fail, the App tells you so and shows you how to
remove it by hand — under that **CINCO** entry, not under a Nona one, because
none exists.

## What is NOT deleted (under either option)

It is worth being clear about what survives deletion, and why:

- **The "Remove ads" purchase**, if you made it. That purchase lives in your
  **Apple or Google** account, not in your Juegos Senda account, so deleting
  your account neither cancels nor refunds it. If you reinstall the App,
  recover it with **"Restore purchases"** on the More screen. Refunds, if you
  want one, are handled by the store where you bought it.
- **Crash reports** your device had already sent us (Firebase Crashlytics).
  These are technical records — device model, system version, error trace —
  that contain no part of your profile, and they are kept for that service's
  retention periods.
- **Usage data** already collected, which is tied to an installation
  identifier and **was never linked to your account**. It deletes itself once
  its retention period is up.
- **Reports**: if you reported someone, or someone reported you, the report
  —who, whom, the reason and the date— is kept for **up to two years** from
  when it was sent, so that it can be reviewed, and then deletes itself. So is
  its copy in our support mailbox, which also carries both players' @username
  and display name as they stood that day; we delete that one ourselves,
  within the same period.
- **Data the law requires us to keep**, typically for accounting or tax
  reasons.

Once the account is deleted, none of the above identifies you, **except the
email copy of a report**, which keeps the @username and display name you had
at the time until we delete it.

## What happens to the data on your phone

Nona also stores your progress on the device itself, so that you can play
offline: the streak, the points, the game in progress, your language and colour
preferences, and the purchase marker.

When you use either of the two options, the App **clears what Nona stores on
this phone** for your account, so that the deletion does not undo itself on the
next launch. Whatever local data remains **disappears entirely when you
uninstall the App**.

## Only want to change something, not delete?

A good deal can be done without permanently deleting anything:

- **Display name, avatar and country** can be edited whenever you like from the
  same profile sheet (More → your profile card). Remember that these three are
  **shared across every game** in the family.
- **Friendships**: you can remove a friend from their row in the Social tab.
- **Blocks**: they are lifted from the same screen where they are applied.
- **Notifications**: they are turned off in your operating system's settings,
  without touching the account.

Your **@username** can be changed without deleting anything: from your
profile, once every 60 days. The previous one stays reserved to you until you
delete the account.

If you want to delete something specific that is not listed here, write to us
at **support@sendapr.com** telling us which data; the same 30-day period
applies.
