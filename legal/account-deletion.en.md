# How to Delete Your Nona Account

**Effective:** 25 August 2026
**Last updated:** 25 August 2026

Nona is part of **Juegos Senda**, a family of games by SENDA LLC that share a
single account. That is why deleting has **two distinct meanings**, and the App
offers you both separately:

- **Remove Nona from your account** — your Nona progress goes. You keep your
  account, your @username, your friends and your progress in the other games.
- **Delete your Juegos Senda account** — everything goes, across **all** the
  games in the family, including those you don't have installed on this phone.

This page explains how to do each one, exactly what is deleted, what survives
and how long it takes.

> **Playing without an account?** Then there is nothing to delete from our
> servers: we create no profile and store none of your games. Everything lives
> on your phone and disappears when you uninstall the App.

---

## Finding the two options

1. Open Nona and sign in.
2. Go to the **More** tab.
3. Tap **your profile card** (the one showing your avatar and @username).
4. At the bottom of the sheet that opens, tap **Delete account**.
5. You will see **both options**, each with its explanation. Choose the one you
   want.
6. To confirm, you will have to **type your @username**. This is deliberate:
   these are actions that cannot be undone, and typing it prevents an
   accidental tap.

The App may ask you to sign in again with Google or Apple before completing a
full deletion. This is not a needless step: it is the security check the account
system requires when your session has been open for a while, and it exists so
that nobody who picks up your unlocked phone can delete your account.

### If you can't get into the App

If you have lost access to your Google or Apple account, or cannot use the App
for any reason, write to us at **support@sendapr.com** from the email address
associated with your account, with the subject:

```
Account deletion request — Nona
```

Tell us whether you want to **leave Nona only** or **delete your entire Juegos
Senda account**. We process these requests **within 30 days** of receiving them,
and usually within the first few. We confirm by email when it is done.

## Option 1 — Remove Nona from your account

This is what the App calls **"Remove Nona from your account"**.

### What is deleted

- **Your Nona progress**: your streak, your best streak, your points — total,
  weekly and monthly — and the record of every daily game you played.
- **Your presence in Nona's leaderboard**: you stop appearing in your friends'
  standings.
- **The marker that you have Nona**: your friends stop seeing you as a Nona
  player, and will be able to invite you again.
- **Your session in this game**: when it finishes, the App signs you out here.

### What you keep

- **Your Juegos Senda account** and your Google or Apple sign-in.
- **Your @username**, which stays yours and is not released.
- **Your display name, your avatar and your country.**
- **Your friends**, across every game.
- **Your progress in the family's other games.**

You can **come back to Nona whenever you like**. You would start from scratch in
this game, with the same identity as always.

---

## Option 2 — Delete your Juegos Senda account

This is what the App calls **"Delete everything"**. It is **immediate and
irreversible**.

### What is deleted

The following is permanently erased from our servers, **across every game in
the family**:

- **Your profile**: display name, avatar, country and the creation date.
- **Your complete progress**: streaks, points and all your games, in **every**
  Juegos Senda game — **including those you don't have installed on this
  phone**.
- **Your friendships and pending requests**, in both directions. Those who were
  your friends stop seeing you in their list and in their standings.
- **Your block list.**
- **Your notification tokens**, so you stop receiving alerts.
- **Your sign-in account**, that is, the record linking your Google or Apple
  sign-in to Juegos Senda.
- **Your "Sign in with Apple" authorisation**, if that is how you signed in.
  When deleting the account we ask Apple to **revoke the permission** you
  granted.

### Your @username is released

When you delete the account, your **@username becomes available again** to
anyone else. If you later create a new account and that name has been claimed,
you will have to choose another: we do not reserve it.

This is the main difference from option 1: **only full deletion releases your
@username**.

### About the Apple permission: it appears as "CINCO"

If you signed in with Apple, there is a detail worth knowing so it doesn't
confuse you: under **Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple**, the
authorisation **does not appear as "Nona", but as "CINCO"**.

This is not a mistake. The Juegos Senda games share a single Apple sign-in, and
the system lists it under the name of the family's first game. It is the same
authorisation for all of them, which is precisely what makes your account the
same across every one.

You normally don't have to do anything: the App asks Apple to revoke it for you.
If that revocation were to fail, the App tells you and shows you how to remove
it by hand — under that **CINCO** entry, not under a Nona one, because none
exists.

## What is NOT deleted (under either option)

It is worth being clear about what survives deletion, and why:

- **The "Remove ads" purchase**, if you made it. That purchase lives in your
  **Apple or Google account**, not in your Juegos Senda account, so deleting
  your account neither cancels nor refunds it. If you reinstall the App, recover
  it with **"Restore purchases"** on the More screen. Refunds, if you want one,
  are handled by the store you bought from.
- **The crash reports** your device may already have sent us (Firebase
  Crashlytics). These are technical records — model, system version, error trace
  — that contain no profile of yours, and are kept according to that service's
  retention periods.
- **The usage data** already collected, which is tied to an installation
  identifier and **was never linked to your account**. It deletes itself when
  its retention period expires.
- **Data the law obliges us to keep**, typically for accounting or tax reasons.

None of the above identifies you as a Nona player once the account is deleted.

## What happens to the data on your phone

Nona also stores your progress on the device itself, so that you can play
offline: your streak, your points, the game in progress, your language and
colour preferences, and the purchase marker.

When you use either option, the App **clears what Nona stores on this phone**
for your account, so that the deletion doesn't undo itself on the next launch.
Whatever local data remains **disappears entirely when you uninstall the App**.

## Just want to change something, not delete?

Much of it can be done without permanently deleting anything:

- **Display name, avatar and country** can be edited whenever you like from the
  same profile sheet (More → your profile card). Remember that those three are
  **common to every game** in the family.
- **Friendships**: you can remove a friend from their row in the Social tab.
- **Blocks**: they are removed from the same screen where they are added.
- **Notifications**: they are turned off in your operating system's settings,
  without touching your account.

Your **@username is the exception**: it is permanent by design, because it is
how your friends find you, and it cannot be changed without deleting the whole
account.

If you want to delete something specific not listed here, write to us at
**support@sendapr.com** telling us which data; we apply the same 30-day period.
